# MathConjectures

Counterexamples and proofs for published conjectures, checked in Lean 4.

| Folder | Result |
| --- | --- |
| [`permanent-partition-conjecture`](permanent-partition-conjecture) | Conjecture 4.27 of Boralevi, Carlini, Michałek and Ventura (*On the codimension of permanental varieties*, Adv. Math. 2025) is false for `4 × 4` and `5 × 5` matrices. |
| [`permanental-component-every-size`](permanental-component-every-size) | For every `n ≥ 5`, the complex scheme cut out by the `(n − 1) × (n − 1)` permanents of an `n × n` matrix has an irreducible component of dimension `n² − 5n + 12`, with a dense open subset where the scheme is smooth and the matrix is invertible. Novelty not established. |
| [`vaserstein-kourovka-16-21`](vaserstein-kourovka-16-21) | Kourovka Problem 16.21 (Vaserstein) has a positive answer over `F₂`: for every `n ≥ 3`, every nonidentity element of `SL_n(F₂)` is a product of `n` conjugates of any nonidentity element. Conditional on two cited theorems of Knüppel and Nielsen (2006) and of Bünger and Nielsen (1999). |
